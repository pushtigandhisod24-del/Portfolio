Care connect is a digital platform that connects seniors with verified local assistants for daily lifestyle, companionship and medical support, while their adult children can coordinate remotely.

The key offerings are: COMPANIONSHIP, TECH ASSISTANCE, HOME SUPPORT AND MEDICAL CARE.

The important highlights of this platform are Vetted caregivers, Flexible bookings, Safety and SOS and secured payments. The target audience is the end users- aged citizens above 65years of age, the primary decision makers who are their adult children who coordinate remotely and who require transparency for their parents.

Embodiment Design was all about incarnation of the core properties, shapes and textures of a natural object, where I happened to choose Jungle Pod.

While exploring this natural object, material and form exploration was necessary too. When the form, shape and texture were being inculcated, I made 'Sede Natura'- Seat of Nature.

This was my final outcome, but through that I had to work on several ideations- where I tried making a Soup Bowl and a Trinket Bowl.

Thenafter, while I was closely observing the shape and structure of the Jungle Pod, it stroked my mind of why not try making a chair which is inspired with jungle pod's shape and moreover texture and color.

Hence, when explored well, I shaped my idea and gave time with iterations and here's the outcome!

This is a built VIRTUAL REALITY experience that depicts how cooking in a 15th Century Portuguese royal kitchen looked like. My team and I worked on the environment , understanding the scale and layouts, VR setup , Interactions, food interactions, audio visuals in the software called Blender and Unity hub.

We chose to build this in VR as a medium for very specific reasons- Procedural knowledge, spatial and material literacy, making absence legible and embodied empathy.
